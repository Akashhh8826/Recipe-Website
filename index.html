Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

  • Milk powder
  • Maida (all-purpose flour)
  • Baking powder
  • Ghee
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Oil or ghee for frying

Procedure

  1. Mix milk powder, maida, baking powder, ghee, and milk to make a soft dough; shape into small balls.
  2. Make sugar syrup by boiling sugar and water.
  3. Deep-fry the balls on low heat until golden brown.
  4. Soak the fried balls in warm sugar syrup for 1-2 hours and serve.

Raj Bhog

Ingredients

  • Chenna (fresh paneer)
  • Cardamom
  • Saffron
  • Sugar
  • Water

Procedure

  1. Knead chenna until smooth and shape into balls.
  2. Boil sugar and water to make syrup.
  3. Cook the chenna balls in the boiling syrup until they expand and become soft.
  4. Add saffron and cardamom, cool, and serve.

Rasmalai

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Lemon juice or vinegar (to make chenna)
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Cardamom
  • Saffron (optional)

Procedure

  1. Make chenna from milk and lemon juice/vinegar; knead and shape into small discs.
  2. Cook the discs in boiling sugar syrup until soft and spongy.
  3. Boil milk, reduce slightly, and add sugar, cardamom, and saffron.
  4. Soak the discs in the flavored milk, chill, and serve.

Shahi Tukda

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Ghee or oil
  • Cardamom
  • Nuts (optional)

Procedure

  1. Fry bread pieces until golden.
  2. Boil milk with sugar and cardamom until slightly thick.
  3. Pour the milk over the fried bread.
  4. Garnish with nuts and serve.
